# SDG Timesheet System — Admin Setup Guide

Administrators use the Admin Dashboard to review attendance, leave, timesheets, waypoints, monthly reports, analytics, and data-management tools. The attendance workspace refreshes live staff and waypoint records; waypoint cards expose staff identity, staff number, timestamp, accuracy, coordinates, address, and cumulative distance.

To review leave, filter by status or staff, open the request, enter a decision reason, and approve or reject. The staff member receives the resulting status and notification history. To review timesheets, use the date, staff, department, and status controls, then inspect task-level details before exporting. PDF exports are suitable for presentation and include check-in and check-out coordinates and addresses; CSV and Excel exports remain available for analysis.

The Data Management tab supports staff and attendance CSV exports plus a JSON backup and restore workflow. Before restoring, review the preview, retain the automatically created pre-restore backup, and type the exact confirmation phrase shown by the interface. Restore actions are admin-only and audited.

For location review, filter waypoints by date or staff name. The activity heatmap summarizes the currently filtered real GPS points, including valid point count, represented staff, average accuracy, and hotspot intensity. Missing or invalid coordinates are excluded rather than converted into artificial locations.
